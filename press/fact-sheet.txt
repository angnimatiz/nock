ONE LINE
Target archery for Apple Vision Pro, played with nothing but your hands.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Raise your bow hand, put the gold inside the sight ring, pinch the other hand to let go. Nock aims the way
real archers do — through a sight ring drawn for your dominant eye — and the arrow flies a true ballistic
path through wind you can see in the flags and hear from the side it blows from.

FEATURES
- Hand tracking is the whole controller: the bow hand aims, a pinch looses the arrow, a restless arm shows at the target.
- Weekly Challenge: everyone in the world shoots the same twelve arrows in the same venue and wind; the week's
  best archer stands in the next lane, arrow for arrow. (Free.)
- A stadium at golden hour, a crowd that rises for the X, and tour venues in Kyoto, Oaxaca and Bergen.
- Set-system matches with a real end clock, an eight-archer knockout tournament, a four-day World Championship
  and a season with a national selection.
- Left-handed archers and either dominant eye are supported. No ads, no tracking, no sign-up.
