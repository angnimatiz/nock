Raise. Draw. Let go.들고, 당기고, 놓는다

  1. 01

    Raise the bow hand

    A sight ring rises on the bow. Put the gold inside it — that is where the arrow goes. A straight arm holds the pin still; a restless one shows at the target.

  2. 02

    Read the wind

    Watch the flags, listen for the side it blows from. The end clock runs red, green and yellow — miss it and the arrow scores M.

  3. 03

    Pinch to loose

    No controller. The arrow flies a real ballistic path into the face, and a stadium at golden hour rises when you find the X.

The sight ring is drawn for your dominant eye, the way it works on a real range. Left-handed archers welcome.
